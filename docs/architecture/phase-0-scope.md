# Phase 0 Scope

Phase 0 is the foundational proof of the Enterprise Financial & Profit Intelligence Infrastructure: a narrowly scoped, enterprise-grade system that proves the platform can take real financial data, establish an auditable economic truth, detect a specific form of financial leakage or variance, calculate its exact monetary impact, preserve the evidence behind that calculation, and present the finding in a form that a finance team can independently verify and act upon. Rather than attempting to support every ERP, every industry, or every financial problem from the beginning, Phase 0 deliberately focuses on one clearly defined business problem—initially a high-value use case such as contract-versus-invoice pricing or discount leakage—using one controlled financial data source such as a Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, SAP, or similar CSV/export. The system will ingest the exported data through a secure and validated ingestion pipeline, preserve the original source data, validate its schema and quality, normalize the relevant financial fields, and create a reproducible internal representation of the transactions without altering the underlying financial truth. It will then apply deterministic financial rules to compare what should have happened according to the available commercial or contractual terms against what actually happened in the transaction data, identify mismatches, quantify the exact financial variance, and calculate the potential economic impact at transaction and aggregate levels. Every detected opportunity will retain its underlying evidence, source references, calculation logic, timestamps, and lineage so that a finance user can trace the result back from the reported ₹/$ amount to the specific transaction and source records that produced it. AI will be used selectively where it provides genuine value—for example, extracting or interpreting relevant contractual information, explaining why a detected variance matters, summarizing the business context, grouping related findings, and making the result easier for a finance professional to investigate—but AI will not be responsible for the financial arithmetic or allowed to invent the monetary result. The financial calculation itself will remain deterministic, testable, reproducible, and independently verifiable. The platform will represent every finding as an Economic Opportunity Record containing the detected issue, affected transactions, evidence, calculation methodology, estimated economic impact, confidence and validation status, business context, recommended next step, and eventual outcome. Finance users will be able to review, challenge, validate, reject, or investigate findings rather than treating every system-generated result as automatically correct. Once a genuine opportunity is validated, Phase 0 will capture whether the customer actually takes action and, where possible, whether that action results in a measurable financial outcome, creating the first link between calculated opportunity and realized economic value. The system will therefore establish the beginning of the controlled lifecycle of detected → evidenced → quantified → validated → acted → measured, while later phases will extend this lifecycle into recommendation, execution, attribution, learning, and broader financial intelligence. Although the product scope is intentionally narrow, the engineering foundation will be enterprise-grade: secure authentication and authorization, tenant isolation where applicable, encrypted data handling, controlled file ingestion, input validation, raw-data preservation, data lineage, reproducible calculations, audit logging, role-based access, secure secrets management, structured application logging, error handling, automated testing, regression datasets, CI/CD, security scanning, dependency management, monitoring, alerting, and controlled deployment. The initial implementation will use a modular architecture rather than prematurely splitting the product into microservices, allowing the core ingestion, normalization, financial-engine, evidence, opportunity, reporting, and audit capabilities to remain independently structured while keeping Phase 0 fast to develop and operate. The first customer-facing output will not be a generic dashboard full of AI-generated insights; it will be a defensible economic finding such as a precisely calculated pricing or contractual variance supported by transaction-level evidence—for example, the ability to prove ₹2.17 Cr of calculated variance and have the customer independently agree with the underlying calculation. The success of Phase 0 will therefore be measured by whether the system can move from raw enterprise data to a financially correct, evidence-backed, independently validated opportunity and ultimately demonstrate that the identified opportunity can lead to real customer action and measurable economic value. Phase 0 is successful when the company has demonstrated, with real data and real customer validation, that its core thesis works: fragmented enterprise data can be transformed into trustworthy economic intelligence that identifies where money is being lost or under-realized, explains exactly why, quantifies the impact, provides evidence that finance teams can trust, and creates a measurable path from calculated variance to realized value. This becomes the technical, product, data, validation, and commercial foundation upon which subsequent phases can expand into additional leakage types, additional enterprise systems, broader financial domains, simulations, recommendations, controlled execution, continuous learning, and eventually the full Enterprise Financial & Profit Intelligence Infrastructure and its AI CFO interface.
